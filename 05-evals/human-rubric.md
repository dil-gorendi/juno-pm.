# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you're scoring:** One Risk radar card from Juno, the Module 4 pillar. The card shows up to 3 Top risks with their signals and 1 next step for each, then Also watch and Data gaps. In Phase 2, the card is on the app page. In Phase 3, Juno posts it each weekday in the private PM channel.
- **Value proposition:** In 1 minute each weekday, the PM sees the open threads that put a customer or a commitment at risk. Each thread has a correct reason and a next step that the PM can do. All facts on the card come from the threads.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No hallucination (deal-breaker) | Does each ID, name, date, number, signal value, and quote of a thread on the card agree with that thread? Do not score the harness lines. | Yes / No | One item is not in the thread, or it has a different value. Or a signal value does not agree with the thread dates. | n/a | Each item agrees with the thread. Each quote is 1 whole sentence of a customer message, word for word. |
| Escalation correct | Does each Top risk have the Escalation flag only when a customer message says that the customer will escalate, cancel, or not renew? | Yes / No | A flag is on a message without this statement, such as "we will not cancel". Or a Top risk has this statement but no flag. | n/a | Each flag has this statement, and each Top risk with this statement has a flag. |
| Right threads | Before you read the card, write your own top 3 of the open threads. How many of your 3 are in Top risks? | 1 to 5 | 0 or 1 of your 3 is in Top risks. | 2 of your 3 are in Top risks. | 3 of your 3 are in Top risks. |
| Next step useful | Can the PM do the next step today, as written, with no need to open the thread? | 1 to 5 | The step does not help with the signal of the thread, or the PM cannot do it. | The step is correct for the signal, but the PM must open the thread to know whom to ask or what to ask. | The PM can do the step today as written. It names whom to ask, by name from the input or by role, and what to ask. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you'll score, and how often:** We score each card of the 10 shadow runs in Phase 2. After that, we score the 5 weekday cards each week, and each Top risk on them. The 10 unflagged threads that the PM reads each week stay as the check for missed Escalations.
- **Who scores them with you:** A second eval reviewer, the support team lead, scores the same cards. The reviewer reads the cards in the run log and the threads in the source of the run. In Phase 2, the source is the CSV export. In Phase 3, it is the support desk. The reviewer does not see my scores. Before the first shadow run, we both score 3 cards from the current skill file and agree on each score.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** A 2 or a 4 is between the two descriptions next to it. If our scale scores differ by 1, we use the lower score. If our Yes / No answers differ, or our scale scores differ by 2 or more, we open the thread together in the same week. We agree on 1 score and add the card to the rubric as an example. If a question causes a disagreement in 2 weeks, one after the other, we write its descriptions again.

## Pass bar

A set of 10 cards passes when 2 conditions are true and no item of the list below occurs. The 10 shadow runs are the first set. After that, each 2 weeks give a set. First, 9 of 10 cards or more have Yes on Escalation correct. Second, the average is 4 or more on Right threads and on Next step useful. Right threads has 1 score for each card. Next step useful has 1 score for each Top risk. A failed set stops the scheduled run. Until a fix passes the eval, the PM clicks Run radar each weekday.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- No hallucination: any "no"
- An email address, a phone number, or a key string (20 or more letters and digits, with no space) on a card. Any change to a thread, or a message outside the private PM channel. A fail of the style test in the PRD fixture set. This test runs in the golden set before Phase 2 and in each regression run. This test gives each statement in 3 styles, and the Escalation flag must not change.
